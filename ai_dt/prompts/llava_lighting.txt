You are looking at a developed photo preview, not an unprocessed RAW file.
Suggest only small corrective shifts relative to what you see.

Reply with one JSON object and no markdown. Use exactly these keys:
- mood: a short phrase of 3 to 8 words describing the light
- rationale: one sentence explaining the correction
- exposure_ev: number from -1.5 to 1.5, positive to brighten
- contrast: number from -0.4 to 0.4, positive to add contrast
- temperature_shift: number from -1 to 1, positive to warm, negative to cool
- tint: number from -1 to 1, positive toward magenta, negative toward green
- saturation: number from -0.4 to 0.4
- shadows: number from -0.4 to 0.4, positive to lift shadows
- highlights: number from -0.4 to 0.4, negative to hold highlights down
- vibrance: number from -0.3 to 0.3

Prefer restraint. A preview that is already well exposed should stay near zero.
Do not invent local brush masks, crop advice, or module names.
